---
title: Sharing a camp
description: Invite camp members and choose personal or shared land.
---

Invite friends to your camp and decide which chunks everyone can use.

## Invite or remove a member

The owner runs `/camp invite <player>`, and the invited player joins with `/camp join <owner>`. A camp can have eight people including its owner. Any member can claim land for the camp. A member can leave with `/camp leave`; the owner can remove someone with `/camp kick <player>`.

## Choose who can use a chunk

Stand in a claimed chunk and use `/claim personal` to reserve it for yourself. Other members, including the owner, cannot build, open containers, or use animals there. Use `/claim shared` to open it to the camp. A member's personal chunks return to shared land when they leave; personal chunks remain personal when the camp becomes a town.

Sharing a chunk does not share its individual locks. See [Managing locks](/homes/managing-locks/) to grant access to a chest or door.
