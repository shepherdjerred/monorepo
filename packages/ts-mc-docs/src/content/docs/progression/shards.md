---
title: Storm Shards
description: Find rare shards and use the windmill to strengthen equipment.
---

Storm Shards are rare drops from mobs and natural ore. Bosses offer better chances than common monsters. Shards are physical items: bring them with you when you upgrade. `/shards` gives an in-game introduction, and `/shards info` describes the item in your hand.
