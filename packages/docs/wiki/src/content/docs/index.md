This wiki is the curated explanation layer for the monorepo. It is deliberately
short, visual, and opinionated.

## What belongs here

- System maps that replace repo archaeology.
- The reason behind infrastructure and architecture choices.
- Operational paths worth remembering.
- Screenshots and diagrams that communicate faster than prose.

## What does not

Raw agent notes, session logs, plans, and TODO records remain available under
[working material](/working/), but they are not the main reading experience.
