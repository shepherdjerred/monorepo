# Red Warfare Search and Destroy kits

Milestone 1 ports the four free kits into `domain/kit/KitBook.java`: Trooper,
Longbow, Shortbow and Rewind. Every kit carries the Blaze Powder "Bomb Fuse" in
hotbar slot 0; a kit's fuse bonus (see `FuseType`) is part of its `KitSpec`.

The remaining eighteen kits and the five killstreaks are catalogued here from
the Red Warfare source (`redwarfare-arcade/src/me/libraryaddict/arcade/game/
searchanddestroy/kits/*.java` and `abilities/*.java`) so milestone 2 can port
them one by one. Prices are credits; "Free" kits have no price. Armor is listed
boots, leggings, chestplate, helmet as the source wrote it. Milestone-2 ports
must keep the module's purity rules: abilities become pure records and
functions driven by events, with randomness from the match seed.

## TODO: killstreaks to port

`KillstreakManager` gives a reward every time a player's kill count reaches a
multiple of the streak's requirement (`kills % required == 0`); the item is
dropped as a falling block the player right-clicks to use.

| Killstreak                             | Kills    | Reward                                                       |
| -------------------------------------- | -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Tracking compass (`CompassKillstreak`) | every 2  | A compass that points at the nearest enemy                   |
| Attack dogs (`WolvesKillstreak`)       | every 4  | A pack of wolves fighting for the player                     |
| Supply crate (`SupplyCrateKillstreak`) | every 4  | A crate of loot (`CrateLootEvent`)                           |
| Iron golem (`IronGolemKillstreak`)     | every 8  | An iron golem fighting for the player (`PathfinderSelector`) |
| Napalm (`NapalmKillstreak`)            | every 11 | A napalm strike on a chosen area                             |

Kill assists count as fractions of a kill (`giveKillstreak` with
`amount < 1`), and the killstreak is announced in the tab list.
