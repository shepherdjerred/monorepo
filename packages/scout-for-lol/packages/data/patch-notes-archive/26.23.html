Team Voice Chat

Team Voice Chat is coming to League this patch in North America and Oceania! It'll be enabled shortly after the patch goes live. We'll roll out to additional regions in future patches as we gain confidence in moderation capabilities and the overall quality of the experience.

  • Team Voice is enabled during champion select and while in-game. Players can be in both Party & Team Voice channels simultaneously.
  • Team Voice is available in all queues except for Classic.
  • Team Voice can only be used with push-to-talk transmission. The default key for Team Voice PTT is "C", with Character Info moved to "Z" and expanding chat history moved to "Ctrl + Z". If these keys are already manually bound, they will not be overwritten, and settings will need to be configured manually.
  • Five voice skins powered by Voicemod are included: Markus, Bexter, Tarys, Raghul, and Jayra. Voice skins are optional modifications that change how others hear you. When enabled, they can be applied to just team voice, or both party and team voice together.
  • Ignore shotcalls from Raghul at your own peril.
  • Added noise suppression to League's voice offerings, defaulted to on.
  • Input gain calibration has been added to the voice option menu in the client. When testing local playback, adjust your microphone's physical position and gain until the calibration ring is inside the shaded area and glows gold. You can also sample how you will sound with voice skins!
  • A report option for voice comms abuse has been added to all reporting menus.
  • Players who are below Honor level 3 or voice restricted from a penalty will be able to join Team Voice to listen , but will not be able to speak in it.

Champions

  NEW   - Talon

This Talon existed from 2011-2016 and was a much more straightforward burst assassin. He's to a targeted blink, a silence, and does not constantly front-flip over walls. He still deals a lot of damage, though.

  • Passive - Mercy: Talon's basic attacks deal increased damage to crowd controlled enemies
  • Q - Noxian Diplomacy: Talon's next attack deals bonus damage. If they're a champion, they additionally take bonus damage over time.
  • W - Rake: Talon throws out blades in a cone that damage and slow enemies on the way out and back in
  • E - Cutthroat: Talon blinks behind his target. If they're an enemy, they're briefly silenced and he deals bonus damage to them for several seconds
  • R - Shadow Assault: Talon turns invisible and sends out a ring of blades that damage all enemies around him. For the next few seconds he gains increased movement speed. Reactivating R or taking any offensive action removes the invisibility and pulls the blades back in, damaging enemies they hit.

  NEW   - Udyr

From 2009 to 2022 this version of Udyr roamed the rift, but for only a short amount of time did he do so with Innervating Locket. Don't worry, Locket is back, so you should buy it and receive your free wins. You should typically build him as an AD-stacker for Tiger Stance or a tanky front-line machine with Phoenix Stance.

  • Passive - Monkey's Agility: Casting an ability gives Udyr a stack of Monkey's Agility, which gives him bonus movement and attack speed, up to a cap
  • Q - Tiger Stance: Udyr gains bonus attack speed for a short time and his first attack will deal bonus damage over time. While maintaining Tiger Stance, his attacks deal bonus damage.
  • W - Turtle Stance: Udyr gains a shield. While maintaining Turtle Stance, he has bonus life steal
  • E - Bear Stance: Udyr gains a burst of movement speed and can run through units. While maintaining Bear Stance, his first attack against each enemy will stun them. He can stun them again after a few seconds.
  • R - Phoenix Stance: Udyr surrounds himself in flames for a few seconds, damaging nearby enemies. While maintainingPhoenix Stance, his first attack, and every third attack thereafter, damages all enemies in a cone in front of him.

  NEW   - Urgot

Urgot initially existed in this form from 2010-2017. Did you know that in the long past, there was an English-language community post talking about the future Urgot rework and mentioned a "Light" and "Dark" form as well as "Shotgun knees." I'm pretty sure that's why the 2017 Urgot rework has shotgun knees, but who's to say? He's somewhat of an AD juggernaut. Manamune is very useful, as are tanky items like Frozen Heart, since his ultimate will place him right inside of the enemy team comp. Please don't swap in the enemy Alistar; it might lose you a game at WCG 2010.

  • Passive - Zaun-Touched Bolt Augmenter: Urgot's attacks and Q debuff enemies he hits, reducing their damage for several seconds
  • Q - Acid Hunter: Urgot fires a missile that damages the first enemy it hits. If it's aimed at an enemy afflicted with his E, it becomes heat-seeking instead of a skillshot.
  • W - Terror Capacitor: Urgot gains a shield. While it holds, his attacks and Q slow enemies.
  • E - Noxian Corrosive Charge: Urgot lobs a grenade that explodes to debuff enemies in the area, damaging them and shredding their armor over several seconds.
  • R - Hyper-Kinetic Position Reverser: Urgot channels onto an enemy, suppressing them and gaining bonus resists. If the channel completes, they swap places and his enemy is slowed for a few seconds.

  NEW   - Xerath

This Xerath version did not last long, only existing from 2011-2014, but has a unique playstyle of becoming a siege tank, blasting enemies from far away while rooting himself in place. He's an AP artillery mage through and through.

  • Passive - Ascended Form: Xerath gains a portion of his ability power as bonus armor
  • Q - Arcanopulse: Xerath fires a beam of enemy, damaging all enemies in a line
  • W - Locus of Power: Xerath locks himself in place, gaining bonus magic penetration and bonus range on his abilities. If he inputs a move command, he ends Locus of Power and gains a short burst of movement speed.
  • E - Mage Chains: Xerath damages and debuffs a target enemy. Landing a spell on a marked enemy stuns them.
  • R - Arcane Barrage: Xerath blasts enemies in an area nearby. Arcane Barrage can be cast three times before going on cooldown.

ARAM: Mayhem

We're back with more content and balance changes for ARAM Mayhem! Four new Headliners are hitting the stage, bringing new ways to play. Whether you're looking for a second chance at life with Arcade, protecting your team with La Ilusion, getting rich with Debonair, or finding the perfect Augment with Maestro, we're excited for you to enjoy the show. 

We've also been keeping an eye on how the new set is playing out and listening to your feedback. Turrets have been falling a little too quickly, barely slowing down enemies and buying little-to-no time for their teams. We're experimenting with turret playing and increasing their defenses so games don't end in a blink. 

Tanks have had a particularly tough time keeping up with the chaos, so we're introducing four new tank-focused Augments to help our beefier champions stay in the fight. We're also making another round of champion balance adjustments to give some struggling picks a helping hand while bringing down a few overperformers. Finally, we're dialing back Bouncing Bees and Boom and the Bass, which have been putting out a little more damage than we'd like. 

As always, thank you for playing and keep the feedback coming!

Systems

Turret Plating

Turrets in Mayhem now have 120 Armor and 120 Magic Resistance, and no longer have their resists lowered as the game goes on. 

Outer and inhibitor Mayhem turrets now have Turret Plating. Plating separates the health bar into thirds, with destroyed plates giving 100g split among the team nearby. On plate destruction, the tower gets a Bulwark buff that grants 60-80 resists per nearby champion.

Replay Download Limit

The replay download limit has been changed from 10 per hour to 50 per day.

A few patches ago we instituted a download limit on replays to prevent large-scale abuse of downloaded replays by bad actors using vibecoded apps to scrape thousands upon thousands of replays. We opted for a time-based limit to still allow creators to make content, while preventing mass exploitative downloads. In order to make replays more accessible for community members that need them while still reducing risk to game integrity, we're shifting to a daily limit.

DJ Sona Interactive Music

We're updating DJ Sona's interactive music to get it more in-line with her original release. The music implementation from the original 2015 version was very resource intensive, and wasn't sustainable long-term. Which is why we released an optimization in 2018. 

But, since then we've made a variety of changes to how we implement music systems in League, and we've improved our optimization methods. Along with the Mythic Variant release, we're updating existing DJ Sona that, while not a 1:1 recreation of the original release, captures the intentions of the original music system. This will add the following features:

  • With each use of Sona's ability, the music will respond by "unlocking" an additional layer, building towards a full track.
  • Upon death, these layers will reset and go away. If you fire off your abilities again, you will build the tracks back up.
  • Upon using your R at levels 6, 11, and 16, your music changes state to play higher intensity playlists.
  • The interactive layers still persist across all levels.
  • We've also fixed the HUD mute and unmute button behavior that had been unreliable and unresponsive to work in similar manners with any other champions with mute-able music.

We're excited to share that this time, we're collaborating with a renowned American electronic music producer and DJ: underscores to bring Sona to the stage collaborating with True Damage, KDA, and Heartsteel.
