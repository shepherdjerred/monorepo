---
title: Worlds
description: The survival worlds, how to reach them, and their borders.
---

Every survival world is permanent; none of them reset. The travel hub at spawn and `/worlds` reach each one, and `/spawn` always brings you back. Every world is on the [live map](https://bluemap.ts-mc.net).

Border radii below are measured from coordinates 0, 0. The two restored maps have round borders; the others are square.

## Frontier

The main survival world, where most players build. `/rtp`, the welcome menu and the starter compass all land here, and the hub's central portal sends you to a random spot in its wilderness.

Border radius: 3,500 blocks. All of Frontier is generated ahead of time.

## Spawn (Main Map 1)

The restored 2015 main world and the server's spawn. Its historical towns are protected. `/rtp world` finds a spot outside them.

Round border, radius 7,000 blocks: the edge of the restored map.

## Main Map 2

The restored 2016 main world. Its historical towns are protected. Reach it from the hub or with `/rtp map2`.

Round border, radius 12,000 blocks: the edge of the restored map.

## Skylands

Floating islands above the void, with modern biomes. Reach it from the hub or with `/rtp skylands`. Falling off an island is a long way down.

Border radius: 5,000 blocks.

## Wilds and Peaks

Wilds has large biomes and Peaks has amplified mountains. Reach them from the hub, or with `/rtp wilds` and `/rtp peaks`.

No border.

## The Nether

Border radius: 3,000 blocks.

## The End

Border radius: 4,000 blocks.
