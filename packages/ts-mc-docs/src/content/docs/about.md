The Storm is a Minecraft community where you can build, explore, trade, and play together. Its worlds connect today's survival play with places built by earlier players.

## A world with a past

The restored 2015 main world is now Spawn, and the restored 2016 main world is also open to explore. Their historical towns are protected. Frontier is the main survival world for new adventures. See [worlds](/survival/worlds/) for ways to reach each one.

The [world archive](/world_downloads/) lets you explore or download snapshots from the server's past, including the main worlds, Skylands, and Creative. It also includes Kargeth, the original city used for the 2014 harbour spawn before The Storm's later changes.

## Find your place

Start with [joining](/join/) and [your first session](/getting-started/), or meet the community on [Discord](/discord/). You can build a [camp or town](/homes/camps-and-towns/), follow [quests](/adventures/quests-and-npcs/), or join a game of [Search and Destroy](/search-and-destroy/).
