---
title: Beginner's Luck
description: Check and understand your early wilderness protection.
---

Beginner's Luck eases your first twelve hours of active play against levelled hostile mobs.

Use `/beginnersluck` to check your progress. AFK and offline time do not count; active minigame time does.

For the first six hours, nearby levelled hostile mobs become level one. Their melee, projectile, and explosion damage to you is reduced by 90%. At most three levelled hostile mobs can gather near you. From six to twelve hours, their levels and damage gradually return to normal.

Nearby players share the lower mob levels, but not your personal damage reduction. PvP, bosses, environmental hazards, and minigames retain their normal rules. Ordinary mobs remain dangerous.

For all twelve hours, you and Spawn Town NPCs cannot damage each other. You can still talk to them, train, and take quests. See [levelled monsters](/survival/advanced/monsters/) for wilderness difficulty after protection ends.
