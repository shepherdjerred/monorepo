---
title: What is different from vanilla?
description: An introduction to The Storm's survival features for Minecraft players.
---

You can gather, craft, build, and explore as usual. The Storm adds shared-world protections, travel, and activities around that familiar survival game.

- **Land and storage:** camps and claims protect building space, and new containers lock automatically. See [your first session](/getting-started/).
- **Travel:** save homes and use commands to visit spawn or explore other worlds. Travel can cost crystals and has cooldowns. See [travel](/survival/travel/).
- **Death and combat:** belongings go into a grave, and PvP is enabled outside spawn unless you opt out. See [graves](/survival/graves/) and [PvP and safety](/survival/pvp/).
- **Community:** townsfolk offer quests and rewards, and players can trade with crystals. Storm Shards can improve equipment. Use `/quests` for your journal and `/bal` for your balance.
- **A shared home:** the worlds are permanent, so other players' builds and choices remain part of the landscape. Check who owns land before building and ask before changing another player's work. See [playing in a shared world](/survival/shared-world/).
- **Sleep:** half of the players in an overworld need to sleep to skip the night; AFK players are excluded unless they are in bed.

For the rules that keep shared worlds enjoyable, read [norms](/norms/).
