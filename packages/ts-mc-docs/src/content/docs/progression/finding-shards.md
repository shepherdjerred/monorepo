---
title: Finding Storm Shards
description: Find ore and mobs that can drop Storm Shards.
---

Find Storm Shards by mining natural ore or defeating mobs in the main overworld, Frontier, Nether and End. The restrictions below apply.

| Source                        | Chance per block or mob | Shards on a drop |
| ----------------------------- | ----------------------- | ---------------- |
| Emerald ore                   | 1%                      | 1–2              |
| Diamond ore or ancient debris | 0.75%                   | 1–2              |
| Lapis ore                     | 0.25%                   | 1                |
| Warden                        | 50%                     | 1–2              |
| Ender Dragon                  | 50%                     | 1–3              |
| Elder Guardian                | 25%                     | 1–2              |
| Wither                        | 5%                      | 1–3              |
| Piglin Brute                  | 2%                      | 1                |
| Evoker or Breeze              | 0.5%                    | 1                |

Deepslate versions of the ores in the table have the same chances as their ordinary counterparts. Other ores and hostile mobs can have smaller chances. A drop is never guaranteed.

Mine natural blocks with the appropriate tool and without Silk Touch. Player-placed and piston-moved ore cannot drop shards, nor can ore in chunks generated before shard tracking began. Spawner and spawn-egg mobs, raids and several other artificial spawns cannot drop them. Trial-spawner Breezes can drop shards.

Bring what you find to the windmill to [upgrade equipment](/progression/shard-upgrades/).
