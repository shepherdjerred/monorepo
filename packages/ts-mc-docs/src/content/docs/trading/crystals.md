---
title: Crystals
description: Check your balance and pay another player.
---

Crystals are the survival currency. You can use them for player shops, towns, and other activities around the server.

- `/balance` shows your balances.
- `/pay <player> <amount>` sends crystals to another player. Check the name and amount before sending.
- `/baltop` shows the crystal balance leaderboard.
