---
title: Town roles
description: Choose members and assistants to help run a town.
---

Town roles determine who can manage land, membership, and the treasury.

| Role      | What they can do                                                                                                 |
| --------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Owner     | Manage the town, rank members, transfer ownership, rename, and delete it.                                        |
| Assistant | Claim and unclaim land, set flags and claim trust, invite players, kick members, and withdraw from the treasury. |
| Member    | Build on town land and deposit into the treasury.                                                                |

The owner can promote a member with `/town promote <player>` and return an assistant to member with `/town demote <player>`. An assistant can kick a member, while the owner can kick an assistant or member. Members can leave with `/town leave`.

See [Camps and towns](/homes/camps-and-towns/) for joining and transferring ownership, and [Claim settings](/homes/claim-settings/) for land access. Locks remain separate from town membership.
