---
title: Sorting items
description: Keep your inventory and containers tidy.
---

Use `/sort` to open sorting settings. Automatic container sorting starts off; `/sort on`, `/sort off`, and `/sort toggle` change it. When enabled, a container sorts as you close it.

To sort immediately, use `/sort inventory`, `/sort hotbar`, or `/sort all`. You can also sneak and punch a chest, barrel, shulker box, or ender chest with an empty hand to sort it.

See [sorting controls](/everyday/advanced/sorting-controls/) for click shortcuts and settings.
