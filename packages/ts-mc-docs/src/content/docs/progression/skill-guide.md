---
title: Skill guide
description: Choose activities that develop each survival skill.
---

Skills improve through survival activities and grant passive benefits as they grow. Check `/skills <name>` to see your current progress. See [Skills](/progression/skills/) for restrictions.

| Skill                          | Practice it by                 | Benefit                                 |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------------------ | --------------------------------------- |
| Mining                         | Mining natural blocks          | Chance of an extra drop                 |
| Woodcutting                    | Cutting natural wood           | Chance of an extra drop                 |
| Excavation                     | Digging natural blocks         | Chance of an extra drop                 |
| Herbalism                      | Gathering plants               | Chance of an extra drop                 |
| Fishing                        | Catching fish                  | Chance of an extra catch                |
| Swords, Axes, Archery, Unarmed | Fighting mobs with that attack | Extra damage to mobs                    |
| Acrobatics                     | Taking fall damage             | Reduced fall damage                     |
| Repair                         | Repairing equipment            | More durability restored per ingredient |

At level 1000, the gathering and fishing bonus reaches a 25% extra-drop chance, combat bonuses reach 10% extra damage against mobs, and Acrobatics reaches 25% fall-damage reduction. Each skill improves independently.

See [Skills](/progression/skills/) for commands, and [Repair equipment](/progression/repair/) for the iron-block method.
