title: Advancements
description: Follow your Minecraft and Storm milestones.
---

The Storm adds milestones to Minecraft's advancements alongside the usual vanilla challenges. Open the advancements screen in your Minecraft client to see your progress.

Storm milestones recognize achievements across the server. Your [quest journal](/adventures/quests-and-npcs/) remains the place to follow quest objectives. Milestones you have already reached can appear without a new announcement.

## Storm tabs

| Tab                | What it tracks                                                  |
| ------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| The Storm          | Homes, quests, towns, and Storm gear                            |
| Tracks             | Progress in the five [progression tracks](/progression/tracks/) |
| Skills             | [Skill](/progression/skills/) milestones                        |
| Spells             | [Spells](/progression/spells/) you have cast                    |
| Mob Arena          | Waves survived in [Mob Arena](/games/mob-arena/)                |
| Search and Destroy | Your [Search and Destroy](/search-and-destroy/) career          |
