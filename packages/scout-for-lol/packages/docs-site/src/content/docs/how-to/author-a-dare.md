---
title: Author and publish a Dare
description: Create a free challenge in Explore, review its contract, and invite the targets to accept.

  order: 16
---

In a beta server with Dares enabled, use `/dare text:<what they should achieve>` to start a private Explore conversation.

1. Describe the achievement, tracked players, queues, and deadline. State explicitly when conditions must happen in the same game.
2. Review Scout's proposed targets, plain meaning, canonical SQL, and historical preview. Ask for revisions until they match your intent.
3. Open **Dares** in the web app, choose the server, and inspect your private draft. The advanced editor and **Revise in Explore** link are available there.
4. Choose **Publish** and confirm the current revision. The public callout lets the targets accept or decline.
5. Once every target accepts, follow progress and evidence from the Dare's detail page. Scout evaluates eligible games against the frozen contract.

You can cancel before activation. Change lifecycle and progress DMs from **Dares → Settings**.

See the [Dares reference](/reference/dares/) for states and confirmation rules.
