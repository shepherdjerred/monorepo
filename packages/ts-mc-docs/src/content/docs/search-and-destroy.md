Search and Destroy is a team game where you fight to be the last team standing or destroy an enemy team's bomb. It is based on the 2013–2014 Red Warfare game and takes place in its own world on The Storm.

Search and Destroy is rolling out and may not be available to every player yet.

## Join a round

Type `/rwf join` to enter the lobby and wait for the next round. Bots fill out the teams, so you can play even if you are the only human.

Pick a kit by right-clicking the nether star in your hotbar. Your first kit is Trooper unless you choose another. Your survival inventory, XP, health and effects are saved and restored when you leave.

## Play

- You have one life. If you die, you watch until the round ends.
- The last team with a living player wins. If the last teams are eliminated at the same time, the round is a draw.
- Each team has bombs. Arm an enemy team's bomb and, if it explodes, that team is eliminated. Defuse your own armed bomb to save your team.
- Combat has no attack cooldown and uses classic knockback; shields and sweep attacks do nothing. Survival combat is unchanged.

Hold the **Bomb Fuse** in your first hotbar slot and right-click a bomb to arm or defuse it. See the [bomb guide](/games/search-and-destroy-bombs/) for timing and other details.

Rounds eventually end with rising poison damage, so keep moving and fight rather than waiting by your bomb.

## Leave or learn more

Type `/rwf leave` or right-click the red dye in the lobby to return to survival. Leaving during a round forfeits its reward, even if you have already died.
