# The Storm — Pinterest creative pack

Four static PNGs, each **1000 × 1500 px (2:3)**. Use these as individual Pins. Nothing has been uploaded or published, and no paid campaign has been created.

All four use The Storm's original cloud wordmark and real archived server screenshots. Dates appear in the artwork and the copy; these images do not represent the current player population or promise that an archived build is in the current world. The Mob Arena's original watermark is retained.

Destination for every Pin: **https://ts-mc.net/**

## Artwork and format

- `preview.png` shows all four designs together; upload the four numbered files individually.
- Sources: the shared original wordmark, January 2015 windmill and Spawn Town captures, and October 2015 Mob Arena capture in the repository's Storm brand assets.
- Typography: Big Shoulders and Work Sans, using the canvas-design skill's OFL font files. No fonts are required to upload the flattened PNGs.
- The images follow Pinterest's [standard image specifications](https://help.pinterest.com/en/business/article/pinterest-product-specs): 2:3, PNG, 1000 × 1500 px. Titles are under 100 characters; descriptions are under 800 characters. All files are under the 20 MB desktop limit.
- Copy follows the existing approved promotion text. It makes no player-count, uptime, reset, economy-balance or new-onboarding promises.
