Mob Arena challenges you and other players to survive waves of monsters with a class kit. You can play alone or with a group.

## Join a game

1. Use `/arena list` to see the available arenas, then `/arena join <arena>` to enter a lobby.
2. Choose a class with `/arena class <class>` and use `/arena ready` when you are ready. Everyone in the lobby must be ready before the countdown starts.
3. Fight through the waves. If you die, you become a spectator while the remaining players continue. Boss waves bring rewards, and later waves grow harder.
4. Use `/arena leave` to leave. Your belongings are restored when you return.

Use `/arena spec <arena>` to watch a game already in progress, or `/arena top <arena>` to see its best runs. The [Discord bridge](/discord/) also posts lobby openings and results.

See [classes and waves](/games/mob-arena-classes-and-waves/) for what to expect during a run.
