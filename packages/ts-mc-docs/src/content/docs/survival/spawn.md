---
title: Spawn Town
description: Get oriented at the windmill and find the people and services at spawn.
---

You arrive at the windmill in Spawn Town. Use `/spawn` to return, and `/guide` to reopen your welcome book. Spawn is PvP-free and its historical towns are protected.

Anna stands beside the windmill. Speak to her for a daily crystal reward, and talk to other townsfolk for quests. `/quests` opens your journal and `/bal` shows your crystals. At the windmill's emerald block, Storm Shards can upgrade equipment while it rains.

When you are ready to explore, use the starter compass, `/rtp`, or `/worlds`. See [your first session](/getting-started/) for a walkthrough and [travel](/survival/travel/) for ways back.
