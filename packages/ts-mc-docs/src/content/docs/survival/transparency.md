Transparency is a core goal of this server. As much as possible is shared
with the community, including [world downloads](/world_downloads/) and
[the code that makes the server tick](https://github.com/orgs/the-storm-mc/repositories).

Our hope is that this will empower the community, and ensure that players
always have access to their creations.

## Gameplay analytics

The Storm can collect private gameplay statistics in PostHog to understand how
many people play, how long they stay, and which features they use. These records
include your Minecraft UUID and current player name, connection times, time
marked AFK, time in survival, arenas or Search and Destroy, and successful feature
interactions such as travel, shopping, quests and spells. Lobby and spectator
time count toward the relevant game mode. NPCs and bots do not count as players.

The gameplay dashboard is available to server operators, not publicly displayed
or used as a player leaderboard. Gameplay events do not contain chat, companion
conversation text, command arguments, coordinates, IP addresses or session
recordings. Website analytics are separate from your Minecraft player identity.
