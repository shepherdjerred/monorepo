---
title: Sharing land
description: Manage camp members, town roles, and access to claimed chunks.
---

Camp members and town members can share land, while personal chunks and locks keep access separate.

For a camp, see [Sharing a camp](/homes/sharing-a-camp/) to invite friends and choose personal or shared land. For a town, see [Town roles](/homes/town-roles/) to choose who can manage it.

## Give access to a chunk

Use `/claim info` while standing in a chunk to inspect it. An owner or assistant can use `/claim trust <player>` to give an outsider access there, and `/claim untrust <player>` to remove it. See [Claim settings](/homes/claim-settings/) for public access and protection options.

Land access does not override a container's lock. See [Managing locks](/homes/managing-locks/) to share an individual container.
