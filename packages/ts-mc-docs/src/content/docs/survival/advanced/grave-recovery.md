---
title: Recover a grave
description: Find your grave and retrieve your belongings safely.
---

Use `/graves` after death to find your graves and prepare a safe return.

1. Make room in your inventory and bring supplies for the journey. A grave appears as a player head near the death site, or near your last safe location after lava or void deaths.
2. Return to the listed location. `/back` may help if the death location is reachable; it has a price and cooldown. See [travel commands](/survival/advanced/travel/).
3. Open the grave to take your belongings. Items that do not fit in your inventory drop at your feet. Experience drops as ordinary orbs rather than entering the grave.

Only you can take its contents for two hours. After that, others can take what fits in their inventory. After seven days, remaining contents drop at the grave and you receive a notice, including on your next join if offline. If no suitable grave location was available, items drop normally instead; check the death site.
