---
title: Seasonal events
description: Find seasonal treats around spawn during Stormnight and Winter Vigil.
---

Seasonal events bring special doors and rewards to the main world around spawn. Visit during the event and interact with the participating doors to collect their surprises.

| Event        | Dates                 | What to expect    |
| ------------ | --------------------- | ----------------- |
| Stormnight   | October 28–31         | Tricks and treats |
| Winter Vigil | December 24–January 1 | Winter treats     |
