---
title: Track levels
description: Compare progression track level descriptions.
---

Each track has five levels. Mechanic, Spellcaster, Governor and chest-shop entries reflect their configured access requirements. The Engineer descriptions and Shopkeeper ads, warps and market priority are shown by `/perks info`, but their availability has not been confirmed. Ask staff before buying a level for those features.

On a narrow screen, scroll the table sideways to see every track.

| Level | Shopkeeper                             | Mechanic                                          | Engineer                                   | Spellcaster                    | Governor                        |
| ----- | -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------ | ------------------------------- |
| I     | Chest shops                            | Switches, cooking pots, glass and bookshelf drops | Crop farm permit                           | Tier I spells                  | Found a town or graduate a camp |
| II    | Market plot and more shops             | Lifts, bridges, gates and doors                   | Breeding permit and farm output conversion | Tier II spells                 | Larger town claim limit         |
| III   | Shop ads and more shops                | Sign copying, painting switching and Stargates    | Logistics networks                         | Tier III spells                | Larger town claim limit         |
| IV    | Shop warp and more shops               | Crush and Bounce pistons                          | Larger networks and more permits           | Tier IV spells, including Ward | Larger town claim limit         |
| V     | Highest shop limit and market priority | SuperSticky and SuperPush pistons                 | Machine-built community projects           | Tier V spells                  | Largest town claim limit        |

See [Tracks and perks](/progression/tracks/) for purchase rules, [Mechanisms](/progression/mechanisms/) for builds, and the [spell catalogue](/progression/spell-catalogue/) for spell tiers.
