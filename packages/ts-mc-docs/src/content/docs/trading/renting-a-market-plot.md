---
title: Renting a market plot
description: Find, rent, and renew an available market stall.
---

Market stalls can be rented when a prepared plot is available and new rentals are open to you.

1. Run `/plot list` to see plot IDs, locations, rent, and current occupancy.
2. If an available rental is open to you, use `/plot rent <id>`. You can hold one rental at a time. The payment covers one week in crystals.
3. Build and stock your shop in the rented plot. See [Player shops](/trading/shops/) for sign setup.
4. Use `/plot renew <id>` to extend your rental. Renewal is manual and remains available when new rentals are closed.

The plot list shows the paid-through time. After it ends, there is a seven-day grace period when you can withdraw your belongings or renew. After grace, the shop is recovered. An in-game message offers a choice between receiving its building materials and stock or a packed chest you can place elsewhere.
