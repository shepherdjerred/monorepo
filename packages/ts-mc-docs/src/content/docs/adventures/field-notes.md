---
title: Field notes and discoveries
description: Find regional field notes and read them in your quest journal.
---

Pick up certain items for the first time to reveal regional field notes in your journal.

<p><a href="/media/quest-field-note/annotated.webp" download>Download annotated image</a></p>

Open `/quests` and visit the collections section to read the notes you have found and see your discovery count. Keep exploring and picking up unfamiliar items to find more notes. The same journal also holds your active quests, quest points, and recent completed quests; see [Quests and NPCs](/adventures/quests-and-npcs/).
