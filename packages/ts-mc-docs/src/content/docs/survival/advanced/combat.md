Prepare for hostile mobs and player combat before leaving Spawn Town.

## PvP and combat tags

Spawn Town is PvP-free. PvP is enabled by default outside spawn; `/pvp off` opts out. You can change your setting once a week.

Damage between players tags both for 15 seconds, including damage from pets, projectiles, TNT, and lingering potions. Teleports are blocked during the tag. Disconnecting or timing out while tagged kills your character; wait for the tag to end before leaving. Kicks and bans do not cause this death.

See [Beginner's Luck](/survival/advanced/beginners-luck/) for early mob protection and [levelled monsters](/survival/advanced/monsters/) for wilderness difficulty.
