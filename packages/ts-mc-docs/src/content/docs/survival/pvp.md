---
title: PvP and safety
description: Understand PvP, combat tags, claims, and container locks.
---

Spawn Town is PvP-free. Outside spawn, PvP is enabled by default; use `/pvp off` to opt out. You can change this setting once a week. During your first 12 hours of active play, Beginner's Luck prevents combat with Spawn Town NPCs, though you can still talk to them and take quests. Ordinary mobs remain dangerous.

Damage between players applies a 15-second combat tag. During the tag you cannot use travel commands, and disconnecting or timing out kills your character. Wait until combat ends before leaving the server.

At night, a bed can help everyone in your overworld: half of the players need to sleep to skip the night. AFK players are excluded unless they are in bed. Bring a bed and supplies when exploring, and remember that ordinary mobs remain dangerous.

Build on your own land or with permission. Use `/claim info` to check the chunk you stand in and `/camp help` to learn about a first claim. New containers lock automatically; look at one and use `/lock info` to check it. Unlocked containers are fair game where a player has access.

Read the [server norms](/norms/) and use `/rules` in game. If something goes wrong, ask in chat or contact staff with `/ticket <what happened>`. If you die, see [graves](/survival/graves/).
