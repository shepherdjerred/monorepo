---
title: Crystals and shops commands
description: Check your balance and find shops.
---

| Command                                                      | Action                                                                                |
| ------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `/balance [player]` (`/bal`)                                 | Check your balance or another player's balance.                                       |
| `/pay <player> <amount>`                                     | Send crystals to another player.                                                      |
| `/baltop [currency]`                                         | View the balance leaderboard.                                                         |
| `/shop`                                                      | List your chest shops.                                                                |
| `/shop toggle`                                               | Toggle shop notifications.                                                            |
| `/shop info` (`/shopinfo`)                                   | Inspect a shop.                                                                       |
| `/shop access`                                               | Toggle between trading at shops whose stock you can open and managing your own shops. |
| `/shop iteminfo [item]`, `/iteminfo [item]`                  | Describe the held item or a named material.                                           |
| `/plot`, `/plot list`, `/plot rent <id>`, `/plot renew <id>` | Find, rent, or renew a market plot.                                                   |
| `/plot confirm`, `/plot cancel`                              | Confirm or cancel a packed-shop placement preview.                                    |
| `/plot reissue <id>`                                         | Reissue a packed shop through mail.                                                   |

See [crystals](/trading/crystals/), [shops](/trading/shops/), and [renting a market plot](/trading/renting-a-market-plot/).
