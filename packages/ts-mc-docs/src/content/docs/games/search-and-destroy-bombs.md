Bombs can eliminate a whole team in [Search and Destroy](/search-and-destroy/).

## Bombs

Every kit puts a Blaze Powder called **Bomb Fuse** in hotbar slot 1. Hold it
and hold right-click on a bomb:

- Hold right-click on an **enemy** bomb to arm it.
- Hold right-click on your **own** armed bomb to defuse it.

Holding runs the clock at full speed for as long as you keep it on the bomb.
A tap still counts, but each tap adds only a fifth of a second, so tapping is
much slower. Arming or defusing starts from a 10-second base. Each distinct
player who works the same bomb, including you, takes one second off, and some
kits carry a fuse bonus. Let go for more than three quarters of a second and
the progress resets. Holding only continues work you started by pressing, so
once a bomb is armed or defused, let go and press again to work it again. The
last living player on a team arms instantly.

An armed bomb explodes after 60 seconds and kills everyone on the team that
owns it. The crater turns nearby blocks to coal.

Some maps also have unowned **nukes**. Anyone can arm a nuke, and when it
goes off it kills everyone except the team that armed it.
