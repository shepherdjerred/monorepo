---
title: Mechanisms
description: Build signs, switches and moving structures with the Mechanic track.
---

Mechanisms add useful interactions to builds. Buy Mechanic levels with `/perks`; see `/perks info mechanic` for the track. You need permission to build at the mechanism and permission to interact where you use it. Put the tag on the **second line** of a sign; tags ignore case and spaces.

See [switches and cooking pots](/progression/switches-and-cooking/), [moving structures](/progression/moving-structures/), [decorating tools](/progression/decorating-tools/), and [special pistons](/progression/special-pistons/) for instructions.

| Mechanic level | Examples                                                                                                                            |
| -------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| I              | `[X]` hidden switch, `[\|]` light switch, and `[Cook]` cooking pot. Glass and bookshelves also drop themselves when broken by hand. |
| II             | `[Lift Up]`, `[Lift Down]` and `[Lift]` elevator signs; `[Bridge]`, `[Gate]` and `[Door Up]` / `[Door Down]` moving structures.     |
| III            | Copy signs with a feather, switch paintings, and build Stargates.                                                                   |
| IV             | `[Crush]` and `[Bounce]` piston signs.                                                                                              |
| V              | `[SuperSticky]` and `[SuperPush]` piston signs.                                                                                     |

## Try a cooking pot

Put a lit campfire one block below a sign and write `[Cook]` on line 2. Right-click the sign with coal to add eight fuel, then right-click it with raw food in your main hand. The cooked items go into your inventory; an empty-hand click reports remaining fuel. A heat source can also be two blocks below the sign.

First-level switches and cooking pots require Mechanic I to use. Elevators, bridges, gates and doors can be used by players without Mechanic II if they have interaction permission. See [elevators and moving structures](/progression/moving-structures/) to build them.

## If a mechanism does not work

Check your Mechanic level with `/perks`, the tag on line 2, and your permission to build or interact at the location. For moving structures, check the specific guide for the required blocks and clear space.
