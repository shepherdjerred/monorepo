In this short walkthrough, you will find a place to build, protect it, and save a home you can return to.

Start by [joining the server](/join/). You arrive beside the windmill in Spawn Town with an iron sword, pickaxe, axe, shovel, and hoe, 16 cooked porkchops, and a welcome book. This starter kit is given once. You also start with 500 crystals.

Choose a biome in the welcome screen, or select **Explore Spawn** to look around first. A compass in your ninth hotbar slot opens the biome picker when you left- or right-click it. It stays with you until your first successful random teleport.

Open the three-page welcome book again at any time with `/guide`. The walkthrough is optional.

## 1. Check your safety settings

Run `/rules`. Spawn is PvP-free, but PvP is enabled outside it. Run `/pvp off` if you want to opt out. You can change that setting once a week.

Beginner's Luck prevents combat with Spawn Town's NPCs during your first 12 hours of active play. You can still talk to them and accept quests.

No griefing is allowed. Unlocked containers are fair game where you have access; locked containers are protected. Read [the rules](/norms/) before leaving spawn.

## 2. Meet Anna

Anna stands beside the windmill near where you arrived. Right-click her and choose her daily reward. You will receive crystals, and `/bal` shows your new balance.

Open `/quests` to see your quest journal. NPCs around town offer more quests when you speak to them.

## 3. Find somewhere to build

Use your compass to choose a biome, or run `/rtp` to travel to a safe wilderness spot in Frontier, the main survival world. Random teleporting is free for your first seven days, although travel cooldowns still apply. `/worlds` reaches [the other worlds](/survival/worlds/).

Gather wood and find an unoccupied place for a small shelter. Build on your own land or with permission. Use `/claim info` to check the chunk you stand in.

## 4. Claim your land

A camp is the usual first home. Start a shelter and the server can create one for you: place a bed, or a chest and a door. You can also run `/camp create`. The chunk you are building in is claimed, and a default home is set if you do not already have one. `/camp help` explains claims, personal chunks, invites, and how long a camp lasts.

A camp holds 16 chunks in one connected shape. Any member can `/claim` the next chunk, and that chunk has to share a full side with the camp. `/claim personal` keeps the chunk you stand in for you alone. `/claim shared` opens it to the camp again.

At Governor I, `/camp graduate <name>` turns the camp into a public town and stops the idle clock. `/town create <name>` still founds a town directly, and it also takes Governor I. `/town list` shows towns, not camps.

## 5. Save your home and protect your supplies

Run `/sethome` at your shelter if a camp did not set one for you. `/home` returns you there later; it costs crystals and follows the travel cooldown. `/tpinfo` shows your current travel prices.

Craft and place a chest. Newly placed containers lock automatically. Look at the chest and run `/lock info` to check its lock. Keep valuable supplies in locked containers.

You now have a claimed place to build, a saved home, and somewhere to store your supplies.

## Keep exploring

Return to the windmill with `/spawn` when you want to meet more NPCs. `/shop` opens the shop catalog; quests and trading earn crystals. Storm Shards can upgrade equipment at the windmill's emerald block while it rains.

For a break from building, `/arena` lists cooperative games and `/rwf join` enters [Search and Destroy](/search-and-destroy/).

If you die, `/graves` lists your graves. Their protection expires, so recover your belongings promptly. Ask in chat if you get stuck, use `/help` for commands, or contact staff with `/ticket <what happened>`.

Join [Discord](/discord/) to meet other players and keep in touch between sessions.
