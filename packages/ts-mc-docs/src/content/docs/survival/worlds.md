Find a place to call home, explore a restored piece of server history, or visit the floating islands. Our survival worlds are permanent and do not reset.

Use `/worlds` to visit Frontier, Skylands, or Main Map 2, and `/spawn` to return to Spawn Town. Nether and End portals lead to their dimensions. You can explore them on the [live map](https://bluemap.ts-mc.net/). Before building, check the land and respect existing work; see [playing in a shared world](/survival/shared-world/).

Every border below is a circle centered at coordinates 0, 0. Measurements are radii.

## Frontier

Our main survival world is a good place to start a home. Most players build here. Use `/rtp`, the welcome menu, the starter compass, or its `/worlds` entry to arrive at a random spot in the wilderness.

Round border, radius 7,000 blocks. Terrain inside the border is generated ahead of time.

## Spawn (Main Map 1)

Visit the restored 2015 main world and Spawn Town with `/spawn`. Its historical towns are protected; `/rtp world` finds a spot outside them.

Round border, radius 7,000 blocks: the edge of the restored map.

## Main Map 2

Explore the restored 2016 main world. Its historical towns are protected. Reach its spawn from `/worlds`, or find a random spot with `/rtp map2`.

Round border, radius 12,000 blocks: the edge of the restored map.

## Skylands

Floating islands above the void, with modern biomes. Reach its spawn from `/worlds`, or find a random spot with `/rtp skylands`. Falling off an island is a long way down.

![A grassy Skylands island beside smaller islands floating over the void.](/screenshots/skylands.webp)

Round border, radius 3,000 blocks.
