---
title: NPC shops
description: Understand server-run shop catalogs and how they differ from player shops.
---

Server-run shop catalogs are separate from player chest shops. Catalog offers can allow buying, selling, or both, and some have daily limits.

`/shop` shows your player chest-shop count and limit; it does not open a catalog for players. For now, use player shops to trade.

For trading available through player signs, see [Player shops](/trading/shops/).
