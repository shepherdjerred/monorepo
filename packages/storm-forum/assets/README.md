# Storm assets

`spawn.jpg` is the archived April 2016 screenshot of The Storm's own Minecraft
spawn, recovered from `The Storm/Assets/site/tsmc_bg_20160418.jpg` in the local
archive. The owned logo and its seasonal variants come from
`packages/storm-theme/assets/` in the public image build. The custom styles
contain independently authored LESS.

`scenery/` contains desktop JPEGs and 960px mobile editions generated with the
built-in imagegen tool from that owned spawn reference. Spring was the reference
for Summer; Summer was the reference for Autumn and Winter, keeping their road,
buildings, flags and skyline consistent. The independently authored pixel-art
SVG garlands in `decorations/` supply the season and festival details.

The prompt set used the `lighting-weather` edit mode: preserve the camera,
Minecraft voxel architecture, central cobblestone road, buildings, black flags,
towers and skyline; change only season and daylight; retain restrained depth of
field and a 16:9 composition; exclude people, UI, text, logos and watermarks.
Spring adds fresh greens and pale blossoms in soft morning light. Summer adds
full green foliage, ripe wheat and warm afternoon sunshine. Autumn adds copper,
gold and rust foliage, fallen leaves and amber late-afternoon light. Winter adds
layered blocky snow, evergreen forms, cool dusk, warm windows and sparse flakes,
while leaving the road visible.

Vendor style archives, sprites, icons and the watermarked historical Halloween
background are not included.
