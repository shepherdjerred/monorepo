League of Legends Patch 26.16 Notes

Champion and system updates, the ninjas have come to Classic, and more!
Game Updates
Riot wroadie, Riot Cashmiir, sternest, Riot Yina
2026-08-11T18:00:00.000Z

 

With this patch, we have a bunch of systems adjustments aimed at some of the strategies that have been emerging in bot lane, as well as opening up some space for itemization diversity in jungle.

Recently we've been seeing increased support roaming really early into the game, to the point where they seem to be operating as a second jungler. While this may be interesting as a strategic option, we feel it is damaging to game integrity when it's the default approach and supports are no longer meaningfully participating in a lane, so we're making higher tradeoffs for this playstyle by tweaking role quest progress for supports and directly addressing specific champions like Camille who have moved into the support role leading this meta.

Related, mages are picking up as a prominent class selection in bot lane in both pro and regular play. While they've been niche but powerful picks for a long time, both from your feedback and the stats, it's clear that it's time for some adjustments to equalize the power here a bit between mages and ADCs. Similar to the approach with supports above, they'll still be viable, just not clearly dominant.

Additionally, we are tweaking the AD fighter itemization space and the jungle pets to encourage a more diverse meta beyond Sundered Sky users to include more AP and Tank junglers. We're excited to see what shakes out next!

SYSTEMS

Classic Gold Generation

League Classic has higher stakes and is generally more snowbally than Season 2026 and that's intentional. We also want players to have a fighting chance at comebacks without adding systems like Objective Bounties that players have to identify and play around. We also don't want to inflate total gold in the game too heavily.

So we're raising the floor on passive gold income and reducing the income of those who farm the best. As a whole, these changes will result in all champions having about 215 more gold passively, with the average laner roughly treading water.

  • Start of Ambient Gold Generation: 65 seconds ⇒ 90 seconds
  • Passive Gold Per 5 Seconds: 8 / 10.5 / 11.5 / 13 at 1 / 10 / 15 / 25 minutes ⇒ 10.5 / 11.5 / 13 at 1 / 15 / 25 minutes
  • Melee Minion Base Gold Value: 20 ⇒ 19
  • Ranged Minion Base Gold Value: 15 ⇒ 14

Classic Homeguards

We want the early game to have high stakes but that doesn't mean a single kill should spell doom for the next 30 minutes straight. As a result, there's a careful balance to strike between properly rewarding the victor for an early kill and helping losing players have something to look forward to. One of our attempts to strike that balance was early game Homeguards, which shipped with Classic last patch. This moderate 10% move speed buff should only shave a second or two off of someone's walk back to lane but your feedback that it feels too modern has been heard. We're keeping the mid-game version of Homeguard, which exists to help winning teams cross the map and continue sieging, but the early game one is being removed. In its place, we're reducing early game death timers by a couple seconds. No new mechanics to learn and winners still get ahead, but losers can get back in there and earn XP and gold to look for their comeback.

  • Homeguard Activation: 0 minutes ⇒ 15 minutes
  • Death Timers: 12 / 14 / 16 / 19 / 22 / 25 / 28 / 30 ⇒ 10 / 12 / 14 / 17 / 20 / 23 / 26 / 29 (levels 1-8)
  • Death Timers At Levels 9+: Unchanged ⇒ Unchanged

Classic Jungle Tuning

When porting over jungle monsters to modern systems, we miscalculated how durable regular jungle monsters would become in the mid and late game. This global health scaling buff will make camps more closely match their histories. Your first clear won't be affected at all, and non-epic monsters will scale up to 5-25% more health than they had before.

  • Maximum Health Scaling: 250% ⇒ 315%

Classic Turrets

Despite having the same effective HP they had in 2013, player feedback has been overwhelming that turrets feel too squishy. And they're certainly much frailer than 2026's norms. As we want turrets to matter and feel important, we agree that they could stand to hold up better, so we're buffing their health by around 15% across the board and nerfing the AP ratio to on-hit damage conversion against towers.

  • Outer Turret Health: 3000 ⇒ 3500
  • Inner Turret Health: 3250 ⇒ 3750
  • Inhibitor Turret Health: 3500 ⇒ 4000
  • Nexus Turret Health: 2600 ⇒ 2750
  • Champion Damage To Turrets: 100% total Attack Damage + 40% Ability Power ⇒ 100% total Attack Damage + 20% Ability Power

Arena

Hey Arenaheads! This Arena patch comes with many champion balance changes, primarily bringing up those that have been struggling for a while so that they get their chance in the spotlight, along with tapping down a few of the most dominant champions.

As for some exciting highlights, Rek'Sai's tunnels will have a 0 second used cooldown when maxed, allowing for some rapid in and out gameplay, Gnar's tired time is being removed so that you're constantly swapping back and forth, and Now You See Me level 2 spawns a clone where you vanished from.

Last but definitely not least, we're bringing back Bravery Arena! For the foreseeable future, Arena will be embracing Bravery and Crowd Favorites every Wednesday. For those that didn't get the chance to play during Bravery weekends, here's the rundown: On Wednesdays, each player will only be able to select Bravery, or choose from their pool of Crowd Favorite champions. The number of Crowd Favorites you're offered is based on how many champions you own.

We know that while a lot of people love Bravery, others enjoy picking from their pool of favorite champions. Our hope is that by having Bravery and Crowd Favorites at a consistent time players will be able to plan when they play to get the experience they are looking for, as well as being open to experimenting with new builds they might not have tried yet.
