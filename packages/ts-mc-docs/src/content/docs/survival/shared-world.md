---
title: Playing in a shared world
description: Build, explore, and share space with other players on The Storm.
---

The Storm's survival worlds are shared and permanent, so your neighbors' homes and projects are part of the landscape too.

A session can be as quiet or adventurous as you like. Gather supplies, visit a neighbor, or set out for somewhere new. At Spawn Town, Anna beside the windmill offers a daily reward. Check `/quests` for your journal or talk to other townsfolk for ideas.

In the main world, `/crier` shares the weather, game time, and a piece of Storm history. `/crier digest` reports today's recorded activity, current weather, and the number of players online in that world.

Head home with `/home`, or use `/rtp` to find wilderness in Frontier. Improve your shelter, explore the [live map](/survival/livemap/), or choose another [world](/survival/worlds/) for your next trip. If a trip goes badly, `/graves` helps you find your belongings; see [graves](/survival/graves/) before setting out to recover them.

Before building, check the chunk with `/claim info`. Build on your own land or ask permission, and give other players' projects room. A claim protects space, but an unclaimed build can still belong to someone. The [first-session guide](/getting-started/) explains how to start a camp.

Leave other players' animals, pets, and villagers alone. Do not grief, harass, follow, or trap players. Unlocked containers are fair game where you have access, while locked containers are off-limits. Your own newly placed containers lock automatically; `/lock info` checks one you are looking at.

PvP is enabled outside protected spawn unless you opt out with `/pvp off`. You can change that setting once a week. See [PvP and safety](/survival/pvp/) and [norms](/norms/) for the rules and what to do if something goes wrong.

Use chat to coordinate a visit or ask about a build. `/tpa <player>` sends a request to visit; the other player chooses whether to accept. See [travel](/survival/travel/) for more ways to meet up.
