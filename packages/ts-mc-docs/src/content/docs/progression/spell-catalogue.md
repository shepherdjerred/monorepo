---
title: Spell catalogue
description: Choose a spell by tier and effect.
---

Buy the matching Spellcaster level to bind a tier's spells. Cleanse, Blink and Carpet also require learning through a quest. Use `/spells info <id>` for current reagents and cooldowns; use the ID with `/spells bind <id>`.

| Tier | ID               | Effect                                                                  |
| ---- | ---------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| I    | `mark`           | Save your position for Recall.                                          |
| I    | `recall`         | Return to your saved Mark.                                              |
| I    | `haste`          | Temporarily move and work faster.                                       |
| I    | `firenova`       | Ignite and damage nearby targets.                                       |
| I    | `cripple`        | Slow and weaken a target.                                               |
| I    | `confusion`      | Turn nearby monsters against one another.                               |
| I    | `roar`           | Draw nearby monsters toward you.                                        |
| I    | `silence`        | Temporarily prevent a targeted player from casting.                     |
| I    | `dowse`          | Extinguish nearby fires.                                                |
| I    | `cleanse`        | Clear poison, withering, fire and frost; quest learning required.       |
| II   | `phase`          | Step through a wall.                                                    |
| II   | `wall`           | Raise a temporary wall where you look.                                  |
| II   | `geyser`         | Lift and damage a target with water.                                    |
| II   | `stealth`        | Fade from sight until the effect ends or you attack.                    |
| II   | `farm`           | Advance nearby crop growth.                                             |
| II   | `forcepush`      | Push nearby targets away.                                               |
| II   | `disarm`         | Knock an item from a target's hand.                                     |
| II   | `shadowstep`     | Move behind a target.                                                   |
| II   | `divine`         | Sense nearby diamond ore.                                               |
| III  | `leap`           | Leap forward and land softly.                                           |
| III  | `dawn`, `dusk`   | Change your personal sky to morning or evening; these share a cooldown. |
| III  | `freeze`         | Freeze a target and nearby water temporarily.                           |
| III  | `drainlife`      | Damage a target and recover health.                                     |
| III  | `blink`          | Teleport toward where you look; quest learning required.                |
| III  | `carpet`         | Create a temporary platform beneath you; quest learning required.       |
| IV   | `entomb`         | Enclose a target in temporary glass.                                    |
| IV   | `purge`          | Damage nearby monsters.                                                 |
| IV   | `stormcall`      | Call lightning where you look; it damages where rain falls.             |
| IV   | `ward`           | Create a temporary bubble that repels monsters.                         |
| V    | `thunderclap`    | Damage and knock back nearby targets.                                   |
| V    | `chainlightning` | Send lightning between nearby targets.                                  |

See [Spells](/progression/spells/) for focus and scroll use.
