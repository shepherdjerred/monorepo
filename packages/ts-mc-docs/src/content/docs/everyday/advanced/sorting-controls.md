---
title: Configure item sorting
description: Choose automatic sorting and inventory shortcuts.
---

Use `/sort` to choose how you sort your inventory and containers.

Automatic container sorting starts off. Use `/sort on`, `/sort off`, or `/sort toggle` to change it. When enabled, a container sorts when you close it.

For an immediate sort, use `/sort inventory`, `/sort hotbar`, or `/sort all`. You can also sneak and punch a chest, barrel, shulker box, or ender chest with an empty hand.

With an inventory open, enabled click shortcuts sort the inventory you click. Middle-click, shift-left-click, double-click, or shift-right-click an empty slot with an empty cursor. Use `/sort hotkeys` to toggle click shortcuts, or `/sort` to adjust individual settings. Sorting respects container access.

To move items into separate containers by type, see [deposit shelves](/everyday/advanced/bank-shelves/).
