Welcome to The Storm, a Minecraft server where you can build, explore, and play with others. Whether you're new to Minecraft or have played for years, there's a place for you here. Java and Bedrock players share the same survival worlds.

**Java:** `ts-mc.net` · **Bedrock:** `mc.ts-mc.net`, port `30004`.

Start with [how to join](/join/), then follow [your first session](/getting-started/) to find a place to build and save a home. In game, `/guide` opens your welcome book. You don't need to register on a forum or finish a tutorial to play.

Take on quests, trade with neighbors, and improve your equipment with Storm Shards. You can also try cooperative arenas or Search and Destroy when you want a different challenge.

- [Survival](./survival/): information about our survival server.
- [Creative](./creative/): how to enter the plot world, claim a cell, and download it.
- [Search and Destroy](./search-and-destroy/): the Red Warfare team
  minigame, its bombs, kits and bots.
- [Norms](./norms/): how members of The Storm are expected to behave.
- [World Downloads](./world_downloads/): explore and download preserved worlds and builds.

Need a hand? Ask in game or meet the community in [Discord](/discord/).
