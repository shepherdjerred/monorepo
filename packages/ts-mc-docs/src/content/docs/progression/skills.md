title: Skills
description: Earn skill experience through everyday survival activities.
---

Skills grow as you play. Use `/skills` to see your levels, `/skills mining` (replace `mining` with a skill name) for progress to the next level, and `/skills top` for the power-level leaderboard. Each skill starts at level 0 and can reach level 1000; your power level is the sum of your skill levels.

![Mining skill progress in chat while looking toward the town across the wheat fields](/screenshots/skill-mining.webp)

The mining command shows the current level and experience toward the next level in chat.

Mining, Woodcutting, Excavation, Herbalism and Fishing reward gathering. Swords, Axes, Archery and Unarmed reward fighting mobs. Acrobatics concerns falling, and Repair improves as you repair equipment. Gathering and fishing can grant extra drops, combat skills can add damage against mobs, and Acrobatics reduces fall damage.

See the [skill guide](/progression/skill-guide/) for activities and passive benefits.

To repair, hold a damaged tool in your main hand and its repair material in your offhand, then right-click an iron block. The material and item must be compatible.

See [Repair equipment](/progression/repair/) for materials and a step-by-step example.

Some activities do not earn experience: gathering player-placed blocks, fertilized flowers and grass, or logs grown from player-planted saplings. Spawner mobs, scripted quest and arena mobs, and non-mob entities do not give combat XP.

## Not earning XP?

Make sure you are in survival mode in a survival world, then try a natural block or naturally spawned mob. Check `/skills <name>` before and after the activity; different skills progress independently.
