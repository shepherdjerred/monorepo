title: Decorating tools
description: Copy sign text and choose a painting with Mechanic III.
---

Mechanic III unlocks two tools for decorating. Buy the level with `/perks` and make sure you have permission at the destination.

## Copy a sign

1. Hold a feather and punch the sign whose text you want to copy. The side facing you is copied.
2. Right-click another sign with the feather to paste onto the side facing you.

Only text is copied. The destination cannot be waxed, and you need build permission there. Pasting a mechanism tag follows the same checks as writing it yourself. Your copied text clears when you leave the server.

## Choose a painting

Right-click a painting to cycle to the next picture of the same size. Sneak and right-click to cycle backwards. You need permission to interact with the painting; if no other picture fits, the painting stays as it is.
