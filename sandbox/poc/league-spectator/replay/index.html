GAMEPLAY EXAMPLES

Known gaps & evidence

Movement byte decoding is compared with RoflLens running the exact executable offline. Position interpolation, relative waypoint interpretation, map variant, and champion facing still need reference playback validation.

Attack and spell packets select real animation clips. Graph variants, blending, attack speed, and cancellation remain approximate. Champion corpses persist until the recorded respawn; NPC corpses currently last four seconds. Minion teams use the recorded spawn side. Champion health and shields use observed values. NPC health, visibility, full spell effects, and equipped skins remain open work.

Missile effects include textured meshes, billboards, and trails. Wards, plants, Shen’s sword, and Bel’Veth summons use recorded spawns. Inhibitor/nexus state remains missing. Buffs use recorded adds/removals; natural expiry and some counter semantics remain unresolved. Diagnostics list unsupported objects and effect features.
