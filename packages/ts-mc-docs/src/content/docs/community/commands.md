---
title: Common commands
description: A quick reference for everyday commands on The Storm.
---

Type a command in chat with its leading slash. Use `/help <command>` for in-game usage. Angle brackets indicate a required value; square brackets indicate an optional value. Some commands depend on your location, role, or resources.

For more commands, browse [help and tickets](/commands/help-and-tickets/), [travel](/commands/travel/), [camps and towns](/commands/camps-and-towns/), [claims, locks, and PvP](/commands/claims-locks-and-pvp/), [crystals and shops](/commands/crystals-and-shops/), [sorting and banks](/commands/sorting-and-banks/), [chat and settings](/commands/chat-and-settings/), [mail and Discord](/commands/mail-and-discord/), [quests](/commands/quests/), [progression](/commands/progression/), [graves](/commands/graves/), [creative](/commands/creative/), and [games](/commands/games/).

| ----------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Read the welcome guide or rules           | `/guide`, `/rules`                 | [Your first session](/getting-started/)                                    |
| Find a place or choose a world            | `/rtp`, `/worlds`                  | [Worlds](/survival/worlds/)                                                |
| Save or visit a home                      | `/sethome`, `/home`                | [Travel](/survival/travel/)                                                |
| Return to spawn or your previous location | `/spawn`, `/back`                  | [Travel](/survival/travel/)                                                |
| Ask to visit a player                     | `/tpa <player>`                    | [Travel](/survival/travel/)                                                |
| Start a camp or claim a chunk             | `/camp create`, `/claim`           | [Camps and towns](/homes/camps-and-towns/)                                 |
| Check your balance or browse shops        | `/bal`, `/shop`                    | [Shops](/trading/shops/)                                                   |
| Open your quest journal                   | `/quests`                          | [Quests and NPCs](/adventures/quests-and-npcs/)                            |
| Find your graves                          | `/graves`                          | [Graves](/survival/graves/)                                                |
| Send a private message or read mail       | `/msg <player> <message>`, `/mail` | [Chat and mail](/community/chat-and-mail/)                                 |
| Find games                                | `/arena`, `/rwf join`              | [Mob Arena](/games/mob-arena/), [Search and Destroy](/search-and-destroy/) |
