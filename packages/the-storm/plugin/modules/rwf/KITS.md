# Red Warfare Search and Destroy kits

Red Warfare shipped twenty-five kits, six of them free: Trooper, Longbow,
Shortbow, Rewind, Medic and Teleporter. Milestone 1 ports the first four into
`domain/kit/KitBook.java`. Every kit carries the Blaze Powder "Bomb Fuse" in
hotbar slot 0; a kit's fuse bonus (see `FuseType`) is part of its `KitSpec`.

The remaining twenty-one kits and the five killstreaks are catalogued here from
the Red Warfare source (`redwarfare-arcade/src/me/libraryaddict/arcade/game/
searchanddestroy/kits/*.java` and `abilities/*.java`) so milestone 2 can port
them one by one. Prices are credits; "Free" kits have no price. Armor is listed
boots, leggings, chestplate, helmet as the source wrote it. Milestone-2 ports
must keep the module's purity rules: abilities become pure records and
functions driven by events, with randomness from the match seed.

## TODO: killstreaks to port

`KillstreakManager` gives a reward every time a player's kill count reaches a
multiple of the streak's requirement (`kills % required == 0`); the tracking
compass is the exception and comes only at exactly 2 kills. Each reward arrives
as an item in the inventory (Ghost and Wraith get it in their second empty
slot). The compass is used by right-clicking anywhere. Every other item is used
by right-clicking a block: the space against the clicked face must be air above
solid ground, and a falling block (wool, wood, iron block or TNT) drops onto it
from at least 50 blocks above the highest block there; the reward happens where
it lands.

| Killstreak                             | Kills     | Reward                                                        |
| -------------------------------------- | --------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Tracking compass (`CompassKillstreak`) | exactly 2 | A compass that tracks the nearest living enemy and warns them |
| Attack dogs (`WolvesKillstreak`)       | every 4   | A pack of wolves fighting for the player                      |
| Supply crate (`SupplyCrateKillstreak`) | every 4   | A crate of loot (`CrateLootEvent`)                            |
| Iron golem (`IronGolemKillstreak`)     | every 8   | An iron golem fighting for the player (`PathfinderSelector`)  |
| Napalm (`NapalmKillstreak`)            | every 11  | A napalm strike on a chosen area                              |

Kill assists count as fractions of a kill (`giveKillstreak` with
`amount < 1`); a reward is checked each time the running total passes a whole
number, and the tab list shows that whole kill count.
