# Studio notes

Observations builders keep coming back to. They are observations, not
instructions: check each against what you see in the render.

## Massing

- The first box is never the building. The second mass (a wing, a tower, a
  porch, a jetty) is what makes the silhouette; add it before any detail.
- Odd widths centre a door; even widths centre a post. Decide which the
  facade wants before the wall goes up.
- A roof break every 7 to 9 blocks of run: a dormer, a cross gable, a step
  in the ridge. Long unbroken ridges read as sheds from every angle.
- Squint. If the squinted render shows one rectangle, no texture will save it.

## Depth

- Relief shows in the relief and normal looks, not in the textured one. A
  facade that is one tone in NORMAL is flat, whatever the blocks say.
- Posts proud by one, windows recessed by one, a sill and a lintel: three
  cheap moves that change the whole elevation.
- Eaves of one or two blocks throw the shadow that separates roof from wall.

## Palette and texture

- Three to five blocks, ordered by value. The frame contrasts the infill;
  the roof contrasts both.
- Mix a surface with purpose: weathering low and near water, moss in shade,
  cracked blocks where feet and weather go. Confetti noise is not texture.
- One accent colour per building; flowers in one or two colours per area.

## Interiors and light

- Cut a floor plan in light mode before promoting. Red cells are where a
  player stands in the dark.
- Ceilings three or more. A two-block ceiling makes every room a corridor.
- Rooms have a job: a trade-specific block or two per room says what it is.

## Site

- No dirt under walls, no floating corners: the foundation follows the
  ground, the ground does not get flattened to the foundation.
- A path to the door, worn where people walk, not a stripe.
- Trees are not lollipops; three sizes, one species per area, leaves kept.

## Process

- Keep the incumbent. A change is accepted when the blind judge prefers it,
  not when it felt like progress.
- Change the lowest axis first, one focused change per iteration.
- Write what you saw in `notes.md`; a resumed session reads it as what an
  earlier self observed, and decides for itself what to do next.
