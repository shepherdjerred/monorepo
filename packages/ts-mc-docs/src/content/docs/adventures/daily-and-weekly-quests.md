---
title: Daily and weekly quests
description: Take repeatable tasks from the quest board and collect their rewards.
---

The quest board offers each player three daily quests and one weekly quest.

1. Visit Tamsin at the quest board in the permanent main world, near coordinates `-38, 64, 2`, and accept a task. Tasks can ask you to hunt creatures or bring materials.
2. Check `/quests` for your active objectives. Nearby players with the same active kill or pickup objective can share credit.
3. Return to the board NPC to hand in your progress and collect the quest reward.

Daily tasks reset at midnight Pacific time. Weekly tasks reset at the start of Monday in the same time zone. See [quest progress](/adventures/quest-progress/) for offers and hand-ins.
